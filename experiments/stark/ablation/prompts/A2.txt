Available tools: get_schema(), search(query, k=5), get_node(node_id), neighbors(node_id, relation, node_type, query, k=20). Call get_schema first to understand node and relation types. Search returns up to 30 BM25 matches with benchmark IDs, names, types, and summary context. get_node returns the complete record including attributes and is_candidate. neighbors examines one hop in either direction, optionally filters relation and node type, and uses keyword matching when query is supplied; k is at most 20. Optional filters may be omitted or empty. Use node_ids from returned records.
